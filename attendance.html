Attendance Overview

The Attendance section allows students to monitor their class attendance for every subject throughout the semester. Regular attendance is an important part of academic success, as it helps students understand classroom concepts, participate in practical sessions, and stay updated with course progress.

Students are encouraged to check their attendance regularly to ensure they meet the minimum attendance requirements prescribed by the institution. Maintaining good attendance also improves academic performance and overall learning experience.

Attendance Rules

  • Students should attend all scheduled lectures and practical sessions.
  • A minimum attendance of 75% is generally required to appear for examinations.
  • Medical or emergency leave should be supported by valid documents.
  • Attendance records are updated regularly by faculty members.
  • Students should report any discrepancies to the concerned department immediately.

Benefits of Good Attendance

  • Improves understanding of classroom concepts.
  • Increases participation in practical and laboratory sessions.
  • Helps students perform better in examinations.
  • Reduces the chances of missing important announcements.
  • Maintains eligibility for semester examinations.

Attendance Tips

Plan your daily schedule carefully and arrive on time for every class. Attend lectures regularly, participate in discussions, and avoid unnecessary absences. If you miss a class due to unavoidable circumstances, inform your faculty member and complete the missed work as soon as possible.

Need Assistance?

If you believe your attendance record is incorrect or have questions regarding attendance policies, please contact your faculty advisor or visit the Contact Us page for assistance.
