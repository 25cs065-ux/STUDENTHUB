  • Enter your registered username.
  • Enter the correct password.

  • Keep your login credentials confidential.
  • Always log out after using a shared computer.

Account Security

Your Student Hub account contains important academic records. Never share your username or password with anyone. Change your password regularly and use a strong combination of letters, numbers, and special characters to keep your account secure.

About Student Hub

Student Hub is an online academic management system designed to simplify student life by providing a centralized platform for attendance tracking, result management, assignment submission, study materials, notices, class timetables, and profile management.

The system improves communication between students and faculty while reducing paperwork and making academic information available anytime and anywhere.
