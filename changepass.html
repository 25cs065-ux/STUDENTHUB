Password Security

Your Student Hub account contains important academic information such as attendance, examination results, assignments, study materials, notices, and personal details. To protect your account from unauthorized access, you should regularly update your password and never share it with anyone.

A strong password helps keep your academic records safe and ensures that only you can access your account. Choose a password that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess.

Password Requirements

  • Password should contain at least 8 characters.
  • Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Include at least one number.
  • Include at least one special character such as @, #, or !.
  • Avoid using your name, date of birth, or phone number.
  • Do not reuse old passwords.

Security Tips

  • Never share your password with anyone.
  • Do not save your password on public computers.
  • Always log out after using Student Hub.
  • Change your password regularly for better security.
  • Report any suspicious activity to the system administrator.

Important Note

After successfully changing your password, you may be required to log in again using your new password. Make sure you remember your updated password before leaving this page.

Need Help?

If you forget your password or experience any problems while updating your account credentials, please contact the Student Hub support team through the Contact Us page.
