Assignment Overview

The Assignments section helps students keep track of all academic work assigned by faculty members. Here you can view the subject name, assignment title, submission deadline, and current submission status. Students are advised to complete their assignments before the due date to avoid late submissions and maintain good academic performance.

Regular completion of assignments improves understanding of course concepts, develops problem-solving skills, and prepares students for examinations. Students should check this page frequently for newly assigned work and important updates.

Submission Guidelines

  • Read the assignment instructions carefully before starting.
  • Submit your work before the specified deadline.
  • Use the prescribed file format if required.
  • Ensure your assignment is your own original work.
  • Review your answers before final submission.
  • Keep a backup copy of your assignment.

Important Instructions

Students are responsible for checking assignment deadlines regularly. Late submissions may result in reduced marks or rejection depending on the faculty guidelines. If any assignment details are incorrect, students should immediately contact the concerned faculty member.

Assignments are an important part of continuous evaluation and help students strengthen their understanding of classroom concepts through practical application and independent learning.

Need Help?

If you experience any issues while submitting assignments or have questions regarding assignment instructions, please contact your faculty member or visit the Contact Us page for technical support.
