Timetable Overview

The Class Timetable provides students with the weekly schedule of lectures, laboratory sessions, and practical classes. Students should refer to this timetable regularly to attend classes on time and prepare the required study materials before each lecture.

Timetables are prepared by the department and may be updated during the semester. Students are advised to check this page regularly for any timetable changes or additional academic activities.

Important Instructions

  • Attend all classes according to the timetable.
  • Carry the required books, notebooks, and laboratory manuals.
  • Reach the classroom at least 5 minutes before the lecture begins.
  • Follow faculty instructions during lectures and practical sessions.
  • Regularly check the timetable for updates or schedule changes.

Benefits of Following the Timetable

  • Improves time management skills.
  • Ensures regular attendance in lectures.
  • Helps students prepare before each class.
  • Reduces confusion regarding lecture timings.
  • Supports better academic performance.
